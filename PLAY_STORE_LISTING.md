FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Imam Raza School Mission is an adult supporter app presenting a proposed education initiative in Karachi, Pakistan.

Explore the mission behind a future school, review its educational priorities, discover original digital sticker collections, and follow the project's progress from its earliest planning stages.

THE SCHOOL VISION

Our proposed school aims to combine modern education, Quran learning and Ahlulbayt-inspired values in an accessible learning environment. The long-term plan includes classes from Nursery through Grade 7, with proposed affordable tuition and scholarship options.

The school is not yet built or operating. Registration, fundraising authorization, land arrangements and educational approvals must be completed before any school can open. The app distinguishes plans from verified achievements.

DIGITAL STICKERS

Preview original sticker art designed around books, learning, nature and curiosity. Collections include Little Learners, Seeds of Knowledge and Bright Minds. Each collection contains original downloadable illustrations intended for personal, non-commercial use after a verified Google Play purchase.

Purchases will become available only after the required technical testing, Play Console merchant configuration and legal approvals. No payments or donations are collected in the current test version.

FOLLOW THE JOURNEY

Browse clearly labelled milestones, from organizing the project and reviewing registration requirements to future site selection, construction planning and school readiness. Progress and financial updates will be provided when supported by verifiable records.

WHY AN ACCOUNT?

Most public project information and sticker previews are available without an account. Optional Google sign-in allows adult supporters to manage their account and, once purchases are introduced, retrieve collections legitimately linked to them. Schoolchildren are not asked to create supporter accounts.

This app contains no advertisements and does not currently accept charitable donations. Digital sticker purchases, when enabled, will be processed only through Google Play Billing.
