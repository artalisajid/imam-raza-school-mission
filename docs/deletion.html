Account & Data Deletion

Updated: 14 October 2026

Google sign-in is configured for a limited test group; payments are disabled. Authorized testers may create a Supabase account when they choose to sign in.

Current preview

To erase the locally saved onboarding preference, open Android Settings → Apps → Imam Raza School Mission → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. If you signed in as a tester, use Account → Account deletion in the app to request deletion of your test account and related profile information.

When accounts launch

The test app has an authenticated self-service deletion endpoint; full public deletion requests and an individual verified support contact will be published before public account registration. Eligible personal profile data and access entitlements will be deleted or anonymized; necessary financial records may be retained for specific legal obligations, as explained in the revised privacy policy.

Contact

We have not yet activated user accounts or an individual privacy request channel. A verified secure contact method will be published before these features go live. Do not disclose identity or payment details in public repository issues.
