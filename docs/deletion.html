Account & Data Deletion

Updated: 14 October 2026

Google sign-in and purchases are not enabled in the current preview. No account is created in Supabase through this Android build.

Current preview

To erase the locally saved onboarding preference, open Android Settings → Apps → Imam Raza School Mission → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. No personal account deletion request is necessary for this preview.

When accounts launch

Before permitting account creation, the released app will provide an in-app deletion request and a secure web-based request method. Eligible personal profile data and access entitlements will be deleted or anonymized; necessary financial records may be retained for specific legal obligations, as explained in the revised privacy policy.

Contact

We have not yet activated user accounts or an individual privacy request channel. A verified secure contact method will be published before these features go live. Do not disclose identity or payment details in public repository issues.
