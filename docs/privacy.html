Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy describes the current preview build and the data features planned for a future version. The school does not yet exist as an operating institution. Google sign-in is being enabled for a small authorized test group; Google Play payments remain disabled.

Who operates this app?

Imam Raza (a.s.) School Mission is an early-stage project proposing to establish an affordable school in Karachi. The project has not represented itself as a registered charity or operating school. An appropriate verified legal identity and dedicated contact channel will be published before accepting payments or opening accounts.

Data collected in the current preview

The current build saves one preference on your device: whether you completed onboarding. It does not create user accounts, collect payment details, upload contacts, or request access to your location, camera or microphone. If a Supabase public-content endpoint is configured, the app retrieves published project updates; standard server and network logs may contain technical request metadata such as IP address.

Google test-user authentication

Authorized adult testers who choose Continue with Google may have a stable Supabase user ID, email address, OAuth provider identifier, optional Google profile display information, encrypted device session tokens and authentication timestamps processed for login, session refresh and account management. Supabase and Google process authentication metadata and may generate normal service logs. Payments remain disabled. We will review Data Safety disclosures before any public release.

Future purchase data

For Google Play digital purchases, we anticipate storing product identifiers, order references, purchase tokens accessible only to trusted services, purchase status, timestamps, currency and prices, entitlement information and refund status. Google Play handles payment instruments; we will not store credit card numbers or CVV codes.

Analytics and communications

The current app has no advertising SDK and does not enable behavioral analytics or push notifications. If introduced later, additional data collection and consent options will be disclosed before release.

Data use and sharing

When enabled, necessary account data may be processed by Supabase for hosting/authentication and by Google for Play purchases and sign-in. We do not sell supporters' data to advertisers. Financial reports shown publicly will use aggregate verified totals rather than personal supporter details.

Security and retention

The planned backend uses least-privilege policies, private asset storage, server-side purchase verification and limited data access. Transaction records may need to be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention and audit obligations. Specific retention periods will be finalized before real accounts or payments are activated.

Children and sensitive data

The app is intended for adult supporters, not children. We do not ask for schoolchildren's records, religious affiliation, income, identity documents or residential address. Future school enrollment information, if needed, will use a separate protected system.

Your choices

You may browse the mission without an account. The local onboarding preference can be cleared using your phone's app-data settings. Authorized test accounts can use the in-app account deletion control once login is available. Necessary anonymized financial records may be retained when legally required. Read the deletion and data-request page.

Changes

We will update this policy whenever the app's actual processing changes, and display a revision date.

Contact and transparency

See the project repository for the current public development status. Do not post personally identifiable or sensitive information in public GitHub issues. A secure individual contact route must be established before account or payment features go live.
